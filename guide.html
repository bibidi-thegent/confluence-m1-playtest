What this is

This is a small, single-player slice of the game's combat: a training ground, three enemy camps and one boss, with two classes to play. We want to find out two things. Can you see every enemy attack coming in time to get out of the way? And do the three ways of aiming (where you face, where your cursor points, and the enemy you've selected) feel responsive and fair? You are testing the game, not being tested. There are no wrong answers, and getting hit a lot is useful to us.

What you need

  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Phones and tablets won't work.
  • A mouse with a right button. You steer by holding the right button, which is hard on a touchpad.
  • About 45 minutes in one sitting.
  • Sound on, if you can. Big attacks play a warning sound when they start.

How to start

  1. Open the game with the Open the game link at the top of this page.
  2. Type your tester name in the Tester box. Use the one Bibidi gave you, such as T03. It goes into your file names.
  3. Pick a class to start with: Bulwark, a sturdy fighter who wants to be close, or Stormcaller, who fights from range. You'll play both.
  4. Click Enter in fullscreen. Fullscreen stops browser shortcuts, such as Ctrl+W, from closing the game by accident. If you leave fullscreen, the Fullscreen button at the top right puts you back.

Setting up saving

Do this once, right after you start:

  1. Press P to open the Playtest panel.
  2. Click Choose telemetry folder and pick an empty folder, for example a new folder called "Confluence playtest" on your desktop. When the browser asks, let it save files there.
  3. That's all. The game now saves your file every 30 seconds and after each fight. Press P again to close the panel.
If you reload the page, choose the folder again: each time you open or reload the game, it starts a new file in that folder. If your browser can't pick a folder, click Download JSON in the same panel at the end, after the survey.

The 45-minute script

Times are a guide, not a race. If a step runs long, carry on; if you finish early, play a little more of that step. Press T to teleport between areas and Y to swap class. Both work only out of combat.

TimeWhat to do
0:00–0:10Training dummies, five minutes per class. Press K to open Loadout and Talents, and try all three ready-made loadouts (presets) for your class, using every button on the dummies. Then press Y to swap class and do the same.
Bulwark: Bastion Standard, Rune Ward, Skirmisher. Stormcaller: Tempest Standard, Arc Grid, Storm Runner.
0:10–0:20Camps one and two, with each class. Teleport to the Open area. Clear camp one (west) and camp two (east) with Bulwark, then swap and clear them again with Stormcaller. A cleared camp comes back about 40 seconds later, once you've moved away from it. From here on, use whichever loadout you like best.
0:20–0:25The Juggernaut, with each class. Teleport to the Juggernaut camp and beat the big armoured enemy once as Bulwark and once as Stormcaller.
0:25–0:40The boss, four attempts. Teleport to the Boss arena gate and walk in. Make three attempts with the class you prefer, then one with the other class. Win or lose, each try counts. If you're defeated, click Release and respawn and teleport back.
0:40–0:45Wrap up. First, a 10-second frame-rate check: press B for the Debug panel, click Stress test, and keep the game window in front until it shows your frame rate. Then fill in the survey (below).

The survey

At the end, press P and scroll to Survey at the bottom of the Playtest panel. Rate five statements from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree):

  • I could tell where each attack would land.
  • I understood why I got hit.
  • Facing abilities felt responsive and fair.
  • Cursor abilities felt responsive and fair.
  • Target abilities felt responsive and fair.

There's a box for anything that felt unfair or hard to read. Please use it: short notes help a lot. Click Submit survey. The game saves right away.

Sending your files

When you're done, open your telemetry folder and send Bibidi every .json file in it, the way Bibidi asked (for example, zip the folder and attach it). Send them all, even small ones: each time you open or reload the game, it starts a new file. If you used Download JSON, send the downloaded files instead.

You can change keys out of combat: open Settings (O) and choose Keybinds. Movement (W, A, S, D), the mouse buttons, the wheel and Esc stay fixed. Out of combat you can also drag the core abilities (1–4) around the action bar to reorder them.

Privacy

Your files hold gameplay data (what you pressed, where attacks landed, your fights and your survey answers) and the tester name you type. They also note your browser version, screen size and graphics card for the frame-rate check. Nothing else: no account, email or location.

Known limits

  • Browser windows inside other apps, such as an app's built-in preview pane, block mouse capture. There, holding right mouse steers with a ring on screen instead of hiding the cursor. Open the game in Chrome or Edge itself.
  • Saving to a folder needs Chrome or Edge. Other browsers can only use Download JSON.
  • Outside fullscreen, the game asks before the tab closes, so a stray shortcut doesn't end your session.
  • The in-game Help (H) has every control and the combat rules, including how to read enemy attacks.
